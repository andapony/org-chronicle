#+TITLE: Chronicle places

* Vicksburg, Mississippi
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:        ent-vicksburg
:KIND:      place
:ALIASES:   Vicksburg
:END:
Confederate river fortress whose fall on 4 July 1863 gave the Union
control of the Mississippi.

* Gettysburg, Pennsylvania
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:        ent-gettysburg
:KIND:      place
:ALIASES:   Gettysburg
:END:
Site of the three-day battle of July 1863 and Lincoln's dedicatory
address at the soldiers' cemetery.

* Fort Donelson, Tennessee
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:        ent-fort-donelson
:KIND:      place
:ALIASES:   Fort Donelson
:END:
Confederate fort on the Cumberland River; its February 1862 surrender to
Grant opened Tennessee to Union advance.

* Grand Gulf, Mississippi
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:        ent-grand-gulf
:KIND:      place
:ALIASES:   Grand Gulf
:END:
River town below Vicksburg; a staging point during Grant's 1863 march
inland from Bruinsburg.

* Point Pleasant, Ohio
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:        ent-point-pleasant
:KIND:      place
:ALIASES:   Point Pleasant
:END:
Ohio River village; birthplace of Ulysses S. Grant.

* St. Louis, Missouri
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:        ent-st-louis
:KIND:      place
:ALIASES:   St. Louis
:END:
Mississippi River city where Grant married Julia Dent in 1848.

* Mount McGregor, New York
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:        ent-mount-mcgregor
:KIND:      place
:ALIASES:   Mount McGregor
:END:
Adirondack retreat where Grant finished his memoirs and died in 1885.
